# PCIe H2F transport correction report

Status: `H2F_CORRECTIONS_PASS_PENDING_INTEGRATION`

This isolated follow-up corrects the two demonstrated H2F failures while
keeping `../pcie_retirement_contract/` and its frozen inputs immutable. The
candidate FIFO keeps the validated final accepted ID-zero B retirement credit,
holds synchronous RX data through arbitrary AXI WREADY stalls, and carries
partial words between PCIe chunks. The candidate controller limits each H2F
chunk to `min(bytes_remaining, 4096, 4096-host_address[11:0])`, advances the
64-bit host address coherently, and rounds only the PCIe read request to a
DWORD while preserving final AXI byte strobes.

## Reviewable source changes

The concrete production artifacts are in `artifacts/`:

- `fpga_v8_pcie_fifo_h2f.v` retains the frozen FIFO parameter and port API.
  Its RX path adds one-cycle RAM-read holding, byte packing across 4092-byte
  intermediate chunks, a final-word strobe mask, and a two-entry AXI W queue
  with reserved synchronous-read slots. Its TX and AXI-read suffixes remain
  byte-identical to the validated retirement candidate.
- `fpga_v8_pcie_dma_controller_h2f.v` retains the frozen controller API and
  changes H2F chunk sizing, DWORD rounding, high-word carry, boundary checks,
  and final-chunk selection. F2H behavior is checked separately against the
  frozen controller.
- `fpga_v8_pcie_dma_h2f.v` and `fpga_v8_pango_pcie_top_h2f.v` are wrapper
  clones with module and child-instance renamings only. They bind the unique
  candidate controller chain without changing vendor IP.

`artifacts/results.json` records 50 candidate cases:

- 46 successful transfers produced 46 final B credits and 46 MSIs.
- Four intentional wrong-BID, held-B, intermediate-SLVERR, and long-stall
  cases produced no completion credit or MSI; each was followed by a reset or
  recovery transfer as specified by the case.
- Aggregate traffic was 9,994 AW handshakes, 159,427 W handshakes, 9,992 B
  responses, 159,429 FIFO pops, 19,587 actual MRD TLPs, and 159,483 SRAM
  writes.
- The cases include host offsets 0, 4, 8, 12, 4092, and 4100; legal DWORD
  alignment is enforced. FPGA AXI destinations are 128-bit aligned.
- Tested byte counts include 4092, 4100, 8188, 8192, 1,228,800, partial tails,
  and repeated commands. The 4092-byte intermediate chunk is generated by the
  actual controller and verified with carried bytes and SRAM write strobes.
- The long-stall case held WREADY for 2,999 cycles while two FIFO results were
  reserved; the following transfer completed exactly after recovery.

`artifacts/negative_evidence.json` preserves the baseline reproduction: the
old candidate loses the singleton W transfer under post-AW W stall and times
out with WREADY asserted before AW routing; its offset-4 4096-byte request
crosses the PCIe 4KB boundary.

`artifacts/candidate/f2h_parity.log` and `artifacts/f2h_parity.json` record
four F2H controller parity transfers covering 305 chunks. The FIFO TX/read
suffix is also byte-identical by the edit manifest. This is a parity check,
not F2H retirement validation.

## Completion, reset, and integration contract

Final accepted ID-zero B with `BRESP=SLVERR` still retires and is classified as
`DeliveryKnownUnknownResponse`; ordered state readback remains required for
host classification. Wrong BID and an abandoned B do not retire. A coordinated
PCIe/controller/FIFO plus AXI fabric/endpoint reset is required for cleanup;
independent PCIe, controller, or CPU resets remain unsupported and are recorded
as such in `results.json`.

For future integration, compile the four production artifacts under their
unique module names and bind `fpga_v8_pcie_fifo_h2f` with its original named
parameters and ports. Keep the actual DMA wrapper chain under the generated
unique names, do not alter generated IP, and exclude the simulation shell.
Run the parent reset-domain audit and platform MSI/ioctl ordering review before
claiming production delivery-known behavior. This report does not establish
hard-IP link behavior, root-complex ordering, board timing, Linux ioctl
semantics, F2H posted-write retirement, or general transport acceptance.
